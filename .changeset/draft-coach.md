Add the draft-coach skill: a coach for blog post and note drafts in a static-site repo. It checks the frontmatter against the repo's own schema, contributing guide, and published posts, ranks what the body is missing, quotes padded lines with shorter alternatives, offers a page preview, and ends with a publish verdict. It edits the draft only when the author asks for a specific change.
